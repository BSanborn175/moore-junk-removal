How do I estimate my gutter length?

You only need to measure the sections of your home that actually have gutters, not the entire perimeter of the house.

  • Estimate each gutter run and add them together.
  • Use a tape measure along the ground beneath each gutter, if it's safe to reach from the ground.
  • Use approximate wall lengths if you know them.
  • If you're unsure, send us your address and photos and we'll help.

Safety: Do not climb onto your roof or use a ladder just to measure your gutters. An estimate is enough for the calculator, and we can confirm the footage before service.

    The Sandhills Challenge

    Pine Needle Gutter Cleaning for Sandhills Homes

    Longleaf pines are part of what makes Moore County look like Moore County. They're also one of the reasons local gutters require attention.

    Unlike large leaves that may fall primarily during one part of the year, pine needles can accumulate gradually and pack tightly inside gutters. Needles can also work their way toward downspout openings and create stubborn drainage blockages.

    Homes surrounded by mature pines may therefore need gutter cleaning more frequently than homes with little tree coverage. If you are also dealing with ground storm debris, we provide comprehensive yard waste and debris removal across the county.

    If your roof is surrounded by pine trees, don't wait until you see water pouring over the gutter during a storm. Regular cleaning is much easier than dealing with a badly compacted gutter or downspout later.

    Warning Signs

    Signs Your Gutters Need Cleaning

    Schedule a gutter inspection or cleaning if you notice:

    • Water spilling over the gutter during rain
    • Pine needles visibly filling the gutter
    • Leaves or plants growing from the gutter
    • Water draining slowly from downspouts
    • Little or no water coming from a downspout during rainfall
    • Debris collecting around downspout openings
    • Sagging gutters caused by accumulated wet debris
    • Water staining around the fascia or roofline
    • You can't remember the last time the gutters were cleaned

    You don't need to wait for an obvious clog. Preventive cleaning is usually the simpler approach.

    Maintenance Schedules

    How Often Should Gutters Be Cleaned in Moore County?

    There isn't one schedule that works for every property. For many homes, cleaning approximately twice per year is a reasonable starting point.

    Properties surrounded by mature pine trees may need service more frequently because pine needles can accumulate throughout the year. Factors include:

    🌲 Number of trees around the home
    🌿 Proximity of branches to the roof
    🍂 Pine versus hardwood tree coverage
    📐 Roof pitch
    🏠 Gutter size and design
    🛡️ Gutter guards
    ⛈️ Recent storms
    💧 Existing drainage problems

    We can help you determine whether occasional cleaning or recurring maintenance makes more sense for your property.
