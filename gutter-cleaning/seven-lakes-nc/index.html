Lake & Forest Setting

Gutter Cleaning for Wooded Seven Lakes Homes

Many Seven Lakes homes sit beneath or close to mature trees. That creates an environment where gutters can collect debris much faster than they would on an open lot.

Pine needles are particularly easy to overlook because they gradually accumulate and settle into the gutter rather than creating one obvious pile. Over time, that material can restrict drainage and contribute to clogs around the downspouts.

Our service removes the accumulated material and gets the gutter system ready to move rainwater again.

Lake & Vacation Homes

Lake Homes and Second Homes Need Gutter Maintenance Too

Properties that aren't occupied every day can be particularly easy to overlook. A clogged gutter doesn't care whether you're there to see it.

For second homes and properties whose owners travel frequently, gutter cleaning can often be coordinated remotely. Before-and-after photographs can provide an easy way to document the service.

Seven Lakes FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does gutter cleaning cost in Seven Lakes, NC?

Seven Lakes properties are priced with our standard Moore County rates: $1.00 per linear foot for first-story gutters and $1.50 per linear foot for second-story gutters, with a $150 minimum service charge. Pine needles and leaves, normal debris removal, accessible downspout clearing, cleanup and debris haul-away are included. Gutter guards that must be removed and reinstalled add $0.50 per linear foot affected.

Do you provide gutter cleaning in Seven Lakes West?

Yes. We serve Seven Lakes West as well as Seven Lakes North, Seven Lakes South and surrounding West End areas.

Do you remove pine needles from gutters?

Yes. Pine needles and pine straw are common gutter debris on wooded Seven Lakes properties.

Do you clean downspouts?

Yes. Accessible downspouts are checked and cleared when needed during service.

Can you clean gutters at a second home when I'm away?

Often, yes. If we have appropriate property access, many exterior gutter-cleaning jobs can be coordinated without the owner being present.

How often should gutters be cleaned on a wooded Seven Lakes property?

Many homes can start with twice-yearly service, but properties beneath dense tree cover may benefit from more frequent cleaning.

Do you haul away the gutter debris?

Yes. Debris removed during service is collected and removed rather than being left in your yard.
